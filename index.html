Privacy Policy

SyncStream · Effective 15 November 2026

SyncStream is a local-network app for sharing and synchronously playing media between devices on the same Wi-Fi network. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no cloud backend. This policy explains what the app accesses and how your data is handled.

No data collection · No ads · No analytics

Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit or sell personal data. SyncStream does not send your media, device information or usage data to the developer or to any third party.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Local network. SyncStream uses the network only to discover nearby rooms and to exchange signaling, video, audio and shared files between devices on the same local network. All media stays on that network. The INTERNET permission is required for local sockets; no internet service is used.
  • Camera. The camera is used only when you choose Scan QR to scan a host's join code. No photos or video are captured, stored or uploaded.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices / Location. Android requires nearby-device permission (Android 13+) or location permission (Android 12L and below) to create or join a local-only Wi-Fi hotspot. SyncStream uses these permissions only for that hotspot feature. It does not read or store your location.
  • Files and media. You choose files with the system document picker. The app reads the selected items to play or share them with the devices you invite. Guests may save a file the host shared to their own Downloads folder. The developer never receives these files.
  • Notifications. While hosting, SyncStream runs a foreground service and shows a status notification so playback can continue while the screen is off.

Storage

Recent items and preferences are stored locally on your device. There is no cloud sync or backup by the app. You can remove this local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Sharing

Media is shared only with devices you explicitly invite or join, over your local network. It is not shared with the developer, advertisers or other third parties.

Children

SyncStream is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.
